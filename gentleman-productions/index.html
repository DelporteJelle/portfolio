The client

Gentleman Productions makes dance and theatre in Merelbeke — live work, staged for an audience, with a back catalogue of past productions that matters to them as much as the next one. Their brand was already settled before I arrived: dark, sparse, a diamond mark, and the line “storytelling in motion”.
