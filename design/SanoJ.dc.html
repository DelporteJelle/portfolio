The client

A wellness practice serving a Dutch-speaking clientele, where the whole business runs on appointments being booked and treatments being understood before someone walks through the door. [EXPAND: who they are, how you came to build it, and whether this was paid work.]

The brief

A site that explains what is on offer and turns interest into a booked appointment, in Dutch, without a receptionist in the loop. [EXPAND: what they were doing before — phone, Instagram DMs, a booking platform they were paying for?]

Constraints

Small-business realities rather than engineering ones: the site has to be findable by someone typing a treatment name into a search engine, load quickly on a phone held in one hand, and stay editable by a person who does not write code. [EXPAND: anything about privacy or health data you had to be careful with.]

What I built

The full customer path: treatments and what each one involves, pricing, an appointment flow, subscriptions for regulars, gift vouchers, and reviews — plus the contact route that puts an enquiry in front of the owner.

Under that, the unglamorous half that decides whether a small business is ever found at all: a generated sitemap, robots rules, and pages that render fast enough to survive a mobile connection in a waiting room. [EXPAND: what you measured — Lighthouse, Core Web Vitals, search position.]
