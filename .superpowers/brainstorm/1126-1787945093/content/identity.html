Visual identity — same layout, three voices

Structure is fixed now: photographic hero → four cards, three across → about. Below, that exact page is dressed three ways. Real typefaces, real palettes. Judge the voice, not the copy.

A

Warm editorial — Fraunces on paper

Cream ground, a soft-serif display face, sans for body copy, mono for metadata. Human and unhurried; your black-and-white photographs sit on it like plates in a book.

C

Dark — the same editorial voice, inverted

Near-black ground, warm off-white type, a sand accent. Black-and-white photographs sit better on dark than on any other ground — they stop being illustrations and start being prints on a wall. The whole page becomes a gallery.

Also worth deciding

Whether the site ships one of these or supports light and dark via a toggle. Supporting both doubles the decisions on every surface — photo scrims, card borders, screenshot treatment — so it's worth doing only if you actually want it. My advice: pick one, commit, and add the other later if it still nags.
