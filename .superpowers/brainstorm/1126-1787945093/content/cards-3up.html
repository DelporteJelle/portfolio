Three across — and what happens to the fourth

Screenshot-led cards, narrowed to a 3-column grid. Four projects into three columns leaves one orphan, so the real question is what row two does. Note the cards are now narrow enough that the description gets one line, ~12 words — the prose has to shrink with them. Hover the cards.

1

Natural wrap — the fourth card just sits there

Four equal cards, three per row, the fourth alone on row two. No cleverness. It reads as an honest list that happens to have four things in it, and the day you add a fifth the grid heals itself.

What narrowing costs

At 3-up on a ~1100px page each card is roughly 340px wide. That's room for a title, one line of description (about 12 words), and two tags — no more. If a project needs three sentences to be interesting, that sentence has to live on its project page, and the card's job becomes getting the click rather than explaining. Worth knowing before you write the copy.
