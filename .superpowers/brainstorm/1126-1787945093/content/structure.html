Structure — three ways to arrange 4 projects

Grey boxes on purpose. No colour, no typefaces, no photography treatment — the only variable here is what lives where and how many pages exist. Each option shows its sitemap and its home page skeleton.

1

Home is the index — one landing, four entries, deep pages behind them

No separate /work page, because with four projects it would be a page containing four lines. The home page is: who you are in two sentences, then the work, then a short about + contact foot. Each project links to a full page.

2

Front door + destination — home is a statement, /work is the catalogue

Home is short and atmospheric: photograph, who you are, two featured projects, a way in. The full index lives at /work and has room to grow past four without redesigning the home page.

3

One page, no navigation — everything is a scroll

Hero, work, about, contact stacked on a single document; nav links are anchors. Projects get a paragraph each inline rather than a page of their own — deep write-ups either don't exist or link out to the repo/README.
