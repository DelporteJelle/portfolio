Project card anatomy

Structure is settled: hero → all four projects → about + contact. Colour and typeface below are neutral placeholders, still open. What you're judging is what a card is made of — and how the in-progress one behaves, since it has no screenshot and no page yet. Hover the cards.

1

Screenshot-led — image on top, 2×2 grid

The familiar shape, done well: greyscaled screenshot, title + year, one line of what it is, stack tags. Reads instantly. Lives or dies on whether your projects photograph well — a CLI tool and a headless library have nothing to show.

2

Type-led — no images at all, 2×2 of typographic blocks

A rule, a number, a large project name, two lines of prose, the stack. The card is typography. Consistent whether the project is a game bot or a parser, never looks empty, and puts your writing — the thing that actually shows judgement — first.

3

Wide rows — figure left, prose right, four full-width entries

A compromise that suits exactly four items: each project gets a full line of the page, a small figure (screenshot or a drawn diagram when there's no UI), and room for two real sentences. Scans like a list, reads like a card.
